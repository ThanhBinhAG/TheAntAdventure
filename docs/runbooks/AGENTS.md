Canonical operator procedures for CRM deployment, recovery, and security-sensitive infrastructure changes.

## Contents
- `PRIVATE-NETWORK-CUTOVER.md` — private Docker-network deployment, validation, rollback, backup, recovery, and key-rotation procedure

## Boundaries
